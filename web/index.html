CYD Game Hub

19 touch games for the 2.8″ resistive-touch Cheap Yellow Display. Plug the board in with a USB cable, press a button, done. Nothing to install.

This browser can't flash over USB. Web Serial is only available in desktop Chrome or Edge. (Firefox and Safari don't support it, and neither do phones.)
The firmware files aren't here yet. Run python tools/build.py once (it needs arduino-cli), or push this repo to GitHub and let the included workflow build and publish it.

1 · Pick your board

Standard CYD

The common ESP32-2432S028R with one USB port (micro-USB). ILI9341 display.

Use desktop Chrome or Edge to flash. Open this page over HTTPS (or http://localhost) to flash.

2-USB CYD

The newer revision with USB-C and micro-USB. ST7789 display. Try this one if the standard build shows a white or garbled screen.

Use desktop Chrome or Edge to flash. Open this page over HTTPS (or http://localhost) to flash.

2 · What happens

  1. Click the button, choose the serial port of your board (often "USB-SERIAL CH340" or "CP210x") and press Install.
  2. Wait for it to reach 100% (about 30 seconds). The board restarts by itself.
  3. On first start it asks you to touch three yellow crosshairs. That calibrates the touch panel; a stylus is more precise than a finger.

Wireless updates

After this flash, you never need the cable again: on the board open Settings → Wi-Fi & updates, pick your network, and press Check for updates. The board downloads new firmware from this site. Boards flashed with an older version need this one USB flash first (it changes the flash layout to make room for updates). Your Wi-Fi password is stored unencrypted on the board.

Clock screensaver

Settings has two clock tiles: Clock shows a full-screen clock and Clock style customises it — digital or analog, 12/24 hour, seconds, date format, colour, brightness, time zone, optional weather (temperature, conditions and today’s high/low for your time zone’s city or a UK/Ireland city you pick, such as Manchester, from Open-Meteo) and automatic start after a period of inactivity. The time and weather are fetched over your saved Wi-Fi.

The 19 games

Every game has Easy / Normal / Hard (tap the difficulty tag in the menu header), with separate high scores for each.

    Troubleshooting

    The port list is empty or the board isn't found

    Use a USB cable that carries data (some are charge-only). On Windows you may need the CH340 or CP210x driver. If connecting still fails, hold the BOOT button on the board while you click the flash button, and release it when "Connecting" appears.

    Colours look inverted (black is white)

    Open Settings in the hub and toggle Invert colours. If the screen is blank or full of noise, flash the other board variant instead.

    Touch lands in the wrong place or is mirrored

    Settings → Calibrate touch. You can also force it by holding BOOT while the board powers up.

    I want the screen the other way up

    Settings → Screen rotation. The hub stores a separate touch calibration for each orientation.

    Does flashing delete my high scores?

    No. Scores and settings live in a separate storage area and survive re-flashing. Use Settings → Clear high scores to reset them.

    Flashing uses ESP Web Tools and runs entirely in your browser; the firmware is served from this same site.
